# Research Countercheck

The assignment owner gives one independent 🔎 Review Sidekick the load-bearing claims, their primary anchors, exact questions, freshness requirement, and source coverage. Leave out the researcher's conclusion and reasoning. The 🔎 Review Sidekick keeps its own review session and returns evidence, not a design or research verdict for the whole assignment.

For each claim:

1. Reopen the primary anchor and identify what it directly supports.
2. Try to refute the claim with a concrete counterexample or contrary primary source.
3. Look for a competing hypothesis that explains the same observations.
4. Check freshness against live sources when the fact can drift.
5. Return `complete | partial | blocked`, the surviving or refuted claim, anchors both for and against, and the exact unfinished coverage or missing access.

The researcher compares this result with the original evidence before synthesis. A partial or blocked countercheck leaves the affected load-bearing conclusion unresolved unless another verified primary anchor settles the gap. The countercheck keeps its judgment in one session and leaves the owning phase's decisions with that phase.
