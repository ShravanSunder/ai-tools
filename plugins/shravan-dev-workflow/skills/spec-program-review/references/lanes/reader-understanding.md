# Reader Understanding

Mission: reconstruct the complete artifact as a human reader, test whether the intended model can be confirmed or corrected, and identify elements that consume attention without helping that work.

Predicate: the mode-complete receipt leaves a concrete material reader-comprehension risk, or the caller explicitly requests a deep reader review with an exact question and scope.

Expected inputs: lane-schema packet, complete target and governing-source set, confirmed goal boundary and accepted requirements set, structural-realization confirmation for `program-only` or `pair`, companion links, and the mode-complete receipt plus its unresolved reader risk or the caller's exact deep-reader question.

Prerequisites: the complete target and source set, confirmed goal boundary, accepted requirements set, and applicable structural-realization confirmation exist in the lane packet; the parent has reduced the mode-complete receipt and named the unresolved reader risk or caller-authorized deep-reader question.

Maximum authority: fresh-context, read-only, candidate-only.

Non-goals: prose taste, arbitrary shortening, semantic redesign, editing, remediation, verdict, planning, or acceptance.

## Reconstruct the Human Model

Follow the selected mode:

```text
specification-only
  accepted requirements -> authoritative Why/What -> observable contract
    -> proof obligation

program-only
  governing obligation -> scenario/contract -> current/proposed call-path delta
    -> owner/state/failure behavior -> proof seam

pair
  accepted requirements -> specification Why/What -> scenario/contract
    -> program-design call-path delta and other How -> proof
```

For specification review, a human must be able to restate who is affected, what is wrong today, the desired outcomes and obligations, negative space, normal and failure behavior, and proof, then point to the exact claim they would correct.

For program review, a human must be able to compare a representative current and proposed entrypoint-to-effect path, or follow proposed-only and see the explicit no-predecessor case, through owner, interface, callers/callees, edge status, state or effect, result/error, failure/recovery, tradeoff, and proof seam, then point to the exact structural assumption they would correct.

Check that each artifact starts with the smallest useful model and reveals detail afterward. Flag missing links, bottom-up detail before the model, copied prose presented as traceability, or a final artifact that requires scratch or review notes to recover accepted meaning. Preserve one compact breadcrumb or artifact map. Scratch may remain optional evidence.

## Apply the Human Deletion Test

Within the authorized unresolved risk or explicit deep-reader scope, test every applicable heading, paragraph, list, table, and diagram:

```text
If this element disappeared, which first understanding, decision, trace,
failure simulation, proof path, or authoritative lookup would become
underdetermined?
```

Flag an element when no consequence exists: repeated purpose or boundary prose; duplicate sibling-role recitals; authoring, review, readiness, planning, PR, release, or documentation-cleanup narration that is not a system obligation; summaries that only repeat the preceding model; decorative diagrams; receipt fields embedded in durable prose; or claims duplicated from another authoritative home.

Flag jargon or an obscure heading only when it prevents a human from locating or restating the decision, relationship, rationale, boundary, example, or correction point. Preserve useful content and propose the smallest clear title or wording. “Requirement realization and proof seams” may become “How Each Requirement Works and How We Verify It.”

Preserve governing-source pointers that affect decisions, scope and negative space, singular ownership, constraints, alternatives and tradeoffs, behavioral interfaces, state and flow, failure/recovery, concurrency, migration, trust decisions, proof seams, and one useful navigation home. Dense or long is not automatically low signal.

For each diagram, state the reader question it appears intended to answer, then compare the visible meaning with the written requirements or design. Check the applicable owners, direction, timing, cardinality, state or effect, normal and error behavior, changed edges, and proof seams. Keep a view when it makes that relationship easier to understand. Flag a decorative or under-specified view with the exact missing or conflicting meaning; valid syntax, successful rendering, or repeated headings are not enough. Route semantically suspect content to the applicable semantic lane rather than redesigning it.

## Calibrate and Return

Each candidate names the exact element, reader behavior unchanged by deletion, surviving authoritative home when duplicated, semantic material to retain, smallest removal or merge, and any semantic route-back. A blanket word ban, length target, mandatory heading form, or “this reads badly” is not a finding.

Overlap boundary: this lane owns disclosure order, human reconstruction, attention cost, process leakage, and redundant content. `artifact-navigation` owns entry paths, link targets, placement, and authoritative homes. Semantic lanes own correctness, completeness, and repair of retained claims.

Return: the lane-schema receipt plus the deletion consequence, duplicate/surviving home when applicable, retained semantic material, and smallest correction for each flagged element.

Stop when every reader-facing element inside the authorized unresolved risk or explicit deep-reader scope has a keep-or-flag basis, or an exact coverage gap is reported. Only a caller-authorized full-artifact audit expands the stop condition to every reader-facing element in the artifact.
