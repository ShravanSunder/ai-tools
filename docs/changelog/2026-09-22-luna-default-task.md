# Luna starts Worker and Sidekick tasks

- Marketplace plugin: `shravan-dev-workflow` `2.27.0`.
- Affected skill: `manage-agents`.
- A Worker or implementation or research Sidekick starts Exact-steps and Complete-direction tasks, including a normal planned implementation, on Luna `high`. Luna `xhigh` is the broader or retry row.
- Sol, Opus, and Grok are not the starting row for that task. Sol `medium` remains for Partial direction or Cross-system work. An existing Sol low session continues.
- Validation: role-table review. Live evals not run.
