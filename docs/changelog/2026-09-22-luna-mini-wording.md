# Luna Mini wording

- Marketplace plugin: `shravan-dev-workflow` `2.26.0`.
- Affected skill: `manage-agents`.
- Mini now means clear, repeatable work whose steps, inputs, result, and checks are already named.
- Luna `medium` or `high` stays the Operator procedure row. Luna `high` or `xhigh` is exact-steps Worker and Sidekick execution, including a focused change with a named done-state.
- Approach, tradeoffs, and unnamed methods stay Balanced or Frontier.
- Validation: wording review against the current role tables. Live evals not run.
